MegaSpoon

Privacy policy and support. Last updated 29 October 2026.

The short version

MegaSpoon has no account, no sign-in, and no server of its own. We do not collect or sell personal information, and there is no advertising or tracking. Your Passport (the app's record of places you've been), your Locker and your settings stay on your phone and in your own iCloud account. Finding places and their opening hours sends your location to Apple Maps and, outside the bundled cities, to OpenStreetMap's public servers, as set out below.

What the app uses, and where it goes

  • Your location. Used to find places near you and to notice when you arrive at one. Searches go to Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy. For opening hours outside the metro areas bundled with the app, your location and the search radius are sent to OpenStreetMap's public Overpass servers, which are run by volunteers and not by us. The request carries no name, account or device identifier, only what any web request carries, such as your IP address. If you turn on Auto-stamp, the app watches for your arrival at the one place you have committed to, and nothing else. Location is never stored beyond the pick it belongs to and is never used for advertising or tracking.
  • Your Passport, Locker, monster portrait and settings. The Passport is the app's record of places you've been. The portrait is the cartoon monster you build in the app; MegaSpoon never uses your camera or your photos. Stored on your phone and synced through iCloud so a new phone picks them up. That data sits in your own Apple account; we cannot read it.
  • Bumping phones. When you bump with a friend, these go straight to their phone over a local connection: your display name and monster portrait, a random ID so their phone knows you next time, the places you've loved, your best countdown score, your duel tally, whether you have Pro and, with Dares on, the dare you chose. On Spoon-feed, or with "Spoon-feed on this bump" left on, the places you've rated or written about go too, with your stars and notes. Joining a friend's pick sends that pick. Nothing goes through a server, and nothing is kept by us. A friend you bump with is remembered on your phone only.
  • Purchases. Handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see payment details.
  • Diagnostics. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting service, and no third-party SDKs.

Age and children

MegaSpoon is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Where the law asks apps to check age (some US states and Brazil, for now), MegaSpoon asks Apple for your age range: under 13, 13 to 15, 16 or 17, or 18 and over. Apple shares only the range, never your birthday. The app keeps it in memory while it's open, never stores it or sends it anywhere, and uses it for two things only: anyone under 18 doesn't see the Drinks choice or bars, and for anyone under 13 the app skips the OpenStreetMap opening-hours lookups that would send their location. Parents and guardians manage age sharing, and approve the app and its purchases, through Apple's Family Sharing.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you. What the app keeps is on your phone and in your own iCloud, and deleting the app and its iCloud data removes it. Your location goes to Apple Maps and, outside the bundled cities, to OpenStreetMap only to find places and opening hours near you, which the app can't do without it. For any question or concern about your data, write to the address below. You can also complain to your privacy regulator: in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office, in Canada the Office of the Privacy Commissioner, in Australia the OAIC.

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the app's update notes say so.

Accuracy

Place and opening-hours information is provided by Apple Maps and OpenStreetMap and may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. Verify details before travelling. Do not use the app while driving. MegaSpoon relies on Apple Maps and OpenStreetMap to find places and opening hours. If those services change, limit or stop what they provide, the features that depend on them, including Pro's, may change or stop working. The app is provided as is, under Apple's standard licence for App Store apps.
