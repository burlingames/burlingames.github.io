MegaSpoon

Privacy policy and support. Last updated 24 October 2026.

The short version

MegaSpoon has no account, no sign-in, and no server of its own. We do not collect or sell personal information, and there is no advertising or tracking. Your passport, locker and settings stay on your phone and in your own iCloud account. Finding places and their opening hours sends your location to Apple Maps and, outside the bundled cities, to OpenStreetMap's public servers, as set out below.

What the app uses, and where it goes

  • Your location. Used to find places near you and to notice when you arrive at one. Searches go to Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy. For opening hours outside the metro areas bundled with the app, your location and the search radius are sent to OpenStreetMap's public Overpass servers, which are run by volunteers and not by us. The request carries no name, account or device identifier, only what any web request carries, such as your IP address. If you turn on Auto-stamp, the app watches for your arrival at the one place you have committed to, and nothing else. Location is never stored beyond the pick it belongs to and is never used for advertising or tracking.
  • Your passport, locker, photo and settings. Stored on your phone and synced through iCloud so a new phone picks them up. That data sits in your own Apple account; we cannot read it.
  • Bumping phones. When you compare tastes or join a friend's pick, your display name, your passport photo, and the places you have marked as favourites are sent directly to the other phone over a local connection. Nothing goes through a server, and nothing is kept by us. A friend you bump with is remembered on your phone only.
  • Purchases. Handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see payment details.
  • Diagnostics. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting service, and no third-party SDKs.

Children

MegaSpoon is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Accuracy

Place and opening-hours information is provided by Apple Maps and OpenStreetMap and may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. Verify details before travelling. Do not use the app while driving. The app is provided as is, under Apple's standard licence for App Store apps.
